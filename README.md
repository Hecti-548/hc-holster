**hc-holster** shows your weapons on your character’s body and lets each player choose where to carry them. Pistols and rifles appear as props that follow your inventory, with draw and holster animations and an option to hide everything under your clothes. This system is created for all players, for police, gangs and citizens.

**Key Features**

* **Weapon props on the ped**: they appear when you get a weapon and disappear when you lose it
* **Choose where to carry pistols**: front, back, waist, hip, chest, thigh or leg
* **Choose where to carry rifles**: back or chest
* **Draw and holster animations** that match the chosen position
* `/holster` menu to change positions at any time
* `/hide` to hide or show all weapons, with a roleplay `/do` message
* **Police jobs** get the hip **holster by default**
* About **70 weapons configured**, with offsets per category in `config.lua`

**For server owners**

* Edit weapons, models, categories and offsets in `config.lua`
* Mark which weapons can be hidden under clothes (`canHide`)
* Translations in Spanish and English

**Dependencies:** qb-core or qbox, ox_lib, ox_inventory

**Installation:** put the folder in your resources, add `ensure hc-holster` after your dependencies, restart.
